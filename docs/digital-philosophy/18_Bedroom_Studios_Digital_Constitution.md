# 18. BEDROOM STUDIOS — DIGITAL CONSTITUTION
**The Master Code for the Digital Sanctuary**
*Version 1.0 — Autumn 2026*

---

## PREAMBLE
This Constitution governs every pixel, interaction, animation, and line of code across all Bedroom Studios digital platforms. We reject both the disposable noise of algorithmic e-commerce and the sluggish pretension of unplayable WebGL gimmicks.

The website is an **interactive architectural sanctum**—where objects command physical presence, light reveals space, and commerce operates with silent, ethical dignity.

---

## THE TEN LAWS OF THE DIGITAL SANCTUM

### LAW 1: DIGITAL OBJECTS OVER FLAT CARDS
Products are physical artifacts with silhouette, mass, and lighting response—never generic thumbnails trapped inside bordered web cards.

### LAW 2: SPACE BEFORE INTERFACE
Atmospheric presence and spatial stillness precede navigation noise. When entering the studio, the user experiences the quiet room before being presented with interface chrome.

### LAW 3: PROGRESSIVE REVEAL
Information is disclosed as interest is demonstrated: emotional silhouette first, tectonic specifications upon inspection, frictionless checkout upon intent. Never dump 40 data points at once.

### LAW 4: MOTION GOVERNED BY MASS AND INERTIA
All movement obeys Newtonian physics. Heavy objects settle slowly; kinetic chains swing with authentic pendulum decay; interfaces slide with rigid architectural precision. Cartoonish bouncing is strictly forbidden.

### LAW 5: HONEST MATERIAL TELEMETRY
Describe objects with verifiable physical metrics: exact weights in grams, dimensions in millimeters, kelvin color temperatures, and aggregate compositions. Ban all empty marketing adjectives.

### LAW 6: PURPOSEFUL FRICTION
Allow moments of pause and tactile anticipation. Turning on a lamp by pulling a chain or exploring a material drawer creates physical memory and reverence for the object.

### LAW 7: SILENT UTILITY IN COMMERCE
The moment a customer decides to buy, brand poetry steps aside for pure, unhindered utility. Prices are transparent in all-inclusive ₹; the cart is lightning fast (< 100ms); checkout is frictionless.

### LAW 8: TACTILITY WITHOUT SKEUOMORPHISM
Communicate the feel of stone, brass, and polymer through macro-photography, natural raking light, and authentic mechanical audio synthesis—never through fake Photoshop bevels or digital faux-textures.

### LAW 9: DEPTH THROUGH LIGHT, NOT DROP-SHADOWS
Spatial depth is generated by the physics of light radiating from a source and falling off radially onto surfaces. Objects closer to the light source catch specular highlights and cast real contact shadows.

### LAW 10: ARCHITECTURAL ACCESSIBILITY & DIGNITY
The experiential brand layer must never obstruct access. Provide instant keyboard bypasses ("Skip to Catalog"), respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and guarantee AAA contrast. Everyone enters the studio with dignity.
