## 2. THE THREE TESTS OF COHERENCE

Whenever a new digital feature is conceived, it must satisfy the **Three Tests of Coherence**:

1. **The Physical Correspondence Test**: Can you identify the physical equivalent of this digital interaction in our Bengaluru workshop? If the answer is "no" (e.g. an infinite spinning carousel or a scratch-to-win discount coupon), the feature is rejected.
2. **The Friction vs. Utility Test**: Does this feature create authentic reverence for the object, or does it merely slow down the user's attempt to purchase?
3. **The Longevity Test**: Will this digital interface feel calm, dignified, and architecturally sound in five years, or will it look like an obsolete 2026 web design trend?
