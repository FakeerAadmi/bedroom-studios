## 2. THE E-COMMERCE INSTRUMENT STACK

### 1. Price Visibility
- Every price is clearly stated in Indian Rupees (`₹`) formatted with standard Indian numbering (`₹1,200`, `₹6,400`, `₹18,500`).
- Prices are **all-inclusive of domestic GST**. What the customer sees on the product page is exactly what they pay.

### 2. Product Variants (Materials & Finishes)
- Variants are presented as physical material swatches, not tiny text dropdowns:
  - `Raw Portland Grey` (solid concrete swatch)
  - `Charcoal Basalt` (dark aggregate swatch)
  - `Sandstone Terracotta` (mineral warm swatch)
- Selecting a variant immediately updates the hero photography to show the exact physical object in that finish.

### 3. The Slide-Out Cart Drawer
- Clicking `ADD TO BAG` or the cart icon smoothly slides out an ultra-clean, full-height cart drawer from the right edge.
- Features:
  - High-res product thumbnail.
  - Material and finish selection summary.
  - Tactile quantity increment buttons (`-` `1` `+`) with micro-spring damping.
  - Transparent shipping calculator (Free domestic shipping on orders over ₹3,000).
  - Immediate `REQUEST ORDER / CHECKOUT` button.

### 4. Made-to-Order & Custom Request Flow
Because Bedroom Studios produces small-batch architectural objects and custom commissions:
- Orders can be completed via standard secure digital payment or routed into our **Bespoke Request Flow**.
- In the Bespoke flow, the customer’s specifications (voltage requirements, custom cord lengths, custom plinth engravings) are transmitted directly to the studio workshop (via Discord webhooks / direct studio liaison) for personal human confirmation.

### 5. Mobile Commerce Ergonomics
- The primary purchase action is permanently pinned to the bottom thumb zone on mobile screens (`h-14` button with high-contrast chartreuse or solid ink finish).
- Touch target sizes are never smaller than **48px × 48px**.
