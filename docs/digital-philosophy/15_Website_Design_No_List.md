# 15. WEBSITE DESIGN "NO" LIST
**The Digital Prohibitions, Anti-Patterns, and Forbidden Tropes**
*Version 1.0 — Autumn 2026*

---

## PREAMBLE
This is an absolute, non-negotiable list of digital anti-patterns. If any proposed layout, PR, pull-request, or digital component exhibits an item on this list, it is rejected immediately.

---

## 1. NAVIGATION & UX PROHIBITIONS

- **NO Mystery-Meat Navigation**: Never use unlabelled, abstract graphic icons that require the user to hover and guess what they do.
- **NO Scroll Hijacking on Content Pages**: Never alter native browser scroll speed, acceleration, or velocity on standard catalog or product pages. (Scroll locking is permitted exclusively on the initial unlit hero threshold before ignition).
- **NO Unskippable Intro Animations**: Never trap the user inside a video or loading animation that blocks access to the catalog.
- **NO Hidden Prices**: Never replace a catalog price with "Price on Request" or "DM for Price" on standard collection objects. Be transparent.
- **NO Marketing Popups & Overlays**:
  - NO "Join our newsletter for 10% off" popups.
  - NO "Someone in Mumbai just purchased a lamp!" fake toast notifications.
  - NO floating conversational AI chatbot bubbles bouncing in the bottom-right corner.
  - NO exit-intent modal takeovers.

---

## 2. AESTHETIC & VISUAL PROHIBITIONS

- **NO Pointless WebGL 3D Blobs**: Never include spinning 3D abstract chrome spheres, floating liquid blobs, or low-poly terrain meshes simply to show off WebGL capabilities.
- **NO Excessive Glassmorphism**: No hyper-frosted, rainbow-bordered glass cards with 40px blur effects stacked on top of each other. Glass is an optical material in physical lamps, not a software aesthetic excuse for poor contrast.
- **NO Cliché Dark-Mode Gradients**: No purple-to-cyan or neon-pink cyberpunk gradients. The digital room is dark obsidian, warm amber, and raw plaster.
- **NO Generic AI-Generated Virtual Rooms**: Never use generic Midjourney/Flux fantasy living rooms with floating architecture as background wallpaper for product listings.
- **NO Giant Hero Type Without Content**: No 120px headlines that say "THE FUTURE OF LIVING" with zero physical objects visible above the fold.
- **NO Tiny, Illegible Grey-on-Grey Text**: Never set secondary body text below 12px or with contrast below WCAG AA standards. Low contrast is not sophistication; it is bad design.

---

## 3. MOTION & INTERACTION PROHIBITIONS

- **NO Bouncy Cartoon Tweening**: Never use over-damped, rubbery spring animations where menus or cards overshoot and bounce three times like children’s toys.
- **NO Typewriter Headline Effects**: Never animate letter-by-letter text typing on screen. It insults the reader's reading speed and causes cognitive fatigue.
- **NO Hover Gimmicks That Displace Content**: A hover effect must never cause layout shift (e.g., expanding card dimensions that push neighboring cards around).
- **NO Loud Sound Effects on Click**: Audio is strictly reserved for the physical pull-chain ignition switch (using subtle, calibrated Web Audio). Never play beeps, whooshes, or chimes on standard page clicks.

---

## 4. COPYCAT PROHIBITIONS

- **DO NOT Copy Apple**: No floating 3D exploded hardware renders spinning as you scroll; no sanitized stark-white laboratory marketing.
- **DO NOT Copy Aesop Directly**: No faux-apothecary brown bottle pastiche or pretentious literary quote dumping without architectural relevance.
- **DO NOT Copy Vitra Directly**: No corporate contract office catalog formatting.
- **DO NOT Copy Scandinavian Pastels**: No soft powdery peach, baby blue, or pale sage minimalism that lacks mineral and architectural weight.
