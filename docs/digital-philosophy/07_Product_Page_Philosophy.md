# 07. PRODUCT PAGE PHILOSOPHY
**The Tectonic Monograph: Balancing Exhibition and Commerce**
*Version 1.0 — Autumn 2026*

---

## 1. THE CORE CRITIQUE OF STANDARD E-COMMERCE PDPs

The conventional e-commerce Product Detail Page (PDP) is an algorithmic transaction machine:
- Square image box on left.
- Title, 5 yellow stars, discounted price, and urgent "ONLY 2 LEFT!" badge on right.
- Generic bullet points about "high quality."
- Giant bright button screaming "BUY NOW."

This template reduces considered industrial design objects to cheap consumer commodities.

At Bedroom Studios, a product page is treated as an **Architectural Monograph & Studio Exhibition**.

---

## 2. THE NINE-PHASE ENCOUNTER SEQUENCE

A visitor does not merely "view an item"; they journey through a deliberate 9-phase sequence that answers every physical and emotional question:

```
THE NINE-PHASE SEQUENCE
│
├── PHASE 1: THE SILENT ENCOUNTER (Full-height hero silhouette, space to breathe)
├── PHASE 2: SPATIAL PURPOSE (Why this object was created; what problem it solves)
├── PHASE 3: GEOMETRIC & OPTICAL FORM (The silhouette, fluting, and light path)
├── PHASE 4: MATERIALITY & WEIGHT (Exact mix, 53-grade cement, C360 brass, grams)
├── PHASE 5: DIMENSIONAL TELEMETRY (Architectural orthographic drawing with mm specs)
├── PHASE 6: PROCESS & MANUFACTURE (Foundry casting, 0.16mm layer toolpaths, cure time)
├── PHASE 7: BEHAVIOR & INTERACTION (Switch sound, rotary resistance, kelvin output)
├── PHASE 8: PRICE JUSTIFICATION (Provenance, batch size, material costs, fair dispatch)
└── PHASE 9: FRICTIONLESS ACQUISITION (Instant add-to-cart, request-order flow, clear shipping)
```

## 3. DETAILED ANATOMY OF THE BEDROOM STUDIOS PDP

### Above the Fold: The Exhibition Horizon
- **Left Column (7 Columns)**: Generous, uncontained hero photography. The object sits on an authentic surface with real contact shadows. The user can toggle between **Mode A (Object)**, **Mode B (In Room)**, and **Mode C (Macro Detail)** with a single keystroke or tap.
- **Right Column (5 Columns - Sticky)**:
  - *Collection Tag*: `FAMILY I — LUMINAIRES` (Mono, uppercase, tracked).
  - *Title*: `The BOOFA Table Lamp` (Newsreader Display, bold, 36px).
  - *Foundry Spec*: `Cast Portland Stone • Fluted Polymer • 2400K LED` (DM Sans, 13px, muted).
  - *Price*: `₹6,400` (Space Grotesk, 22px, bold, Indian comma formatting).
  - *Lead Time*: `Handcrafted to order in Bengaluru • Dispatches in 4–6 working days`.
  - *Primary Action*: Solid ink button `REQUEST ORDER` or `ADD TO BAG`.
  - *Secondary Action*: Discreet `SAVE TO WISHLIST` icon button.

### Mid-Page: The Tectonic Monograph
As the user scrolls down, the page transitions from exhibition into technical documentation:
1. **The Architectural Essay**: A 150-word narrative written by the designer explaining the spatial tension that birthed this object.
2. **Orthographic Wireframe**: A crisp, 1px architectural vector line drawing showing Front Elevation, Side Elevation, and Top Plan with dimensions indicated in millimeters.
3. **The Material Matrix**: An interactive 3-card breakdown:
   - *The Plinth*: 53-grade Portland aggregate, cured for 48 hours in water, sealed with matte siloxane. Weight: 920g.
   - *The Diffuser*: Continuous perimeter vase-mode PETG, 0.16mm layer height. Heat tolerance: 75°C.
   - *The Hardware*: C360 solid turned brass finial and pull-chain, unlacquered.

## 4. BALANCING EDITORIAL DEPTH AND COMMERCIAL CLARITY

We never force a customer who already knows what they want to read an essay just to make a purchase:
- **The Persistent Sticky Purchase Dock**: Once the user scrolls past the top fold, a discreet, ultra-thin bottom dock or sticky header bar surfaces:
  `[Small Object Thumbnail] [Title] [₹6,400] ─── [ADD TO BAG]`
- This ensures that whether someone is an impulsive repeat buyer or a contemplative first-time collector studying the architecture, the purchase is always exactly one click away.
