# 10. PHOTOGRAPHY AND ART DIRECTION
**The Visual Capture of Mineral Density and Calibrated Light**
*Version 1.0 — Autumn 2026*

---

## 1. THE TRIPTYCH CAPTURE SYSTEM (THREE MODES)

Every product in the Bedroom Studios catalog must be photographed in three distinct visual modes. A product is incomplete if any mode is missing:

```
THE THREE MODES OF CAPTURE
│
├── MODE A: THE TECTONIC OBJECT (Isolated Studio Documentation)
│   └── Neutral warm studio background, precise orthographic angles, clean silhouette.
│
├── MODE B: THE INHABITED ENVIRONMENT (Architectural Context & Scale)
│   └── The object in a real dusk workspace, resting on oiled timber, illuminated.
│
└── MODE C: THE MICRO-TACTILE DETAIL (Macro-Craftsmanship & Materiality)
    └── 1:1 macro focus on toolpaths, cement voids, brass brush grain, and switch contacts.
```

---

## 2. DETAILED SPECIFICATIONS FOR EACH MODE

### MODE A — THE TECTONIC OBJECT
- **Purpose**: Pure product appraisal, dimension verification, and material comparison.
- **Background**: Solid, untextured warm off-white or dark charcoal studio sweep (RGB: `#F4F2EC` or `#141518`).
- **Lighting**: Soft directional key light from 45° left, subtle bounce card fill from right. Light must rake across the surface to reveal the 3D-print fluting and concrete texture.
- **Angles**: Exactly three standard orthographic perspectives:
  1. *Hero 3/4 Perspective* (slightly elevated, 15° down-angle).
  2. *True Front Elevation* (flat horizon plane, zero tilt).
  3. *True Top Plan* (90° overhead showing interior cavity/tray compartments).
- **Shadows**: Natural soft contact shadow directly under the base; never artificial Photoshop Gaussian drops.

### MODE B — THE INHABITED ENVIRONMENT
- **Purpose**: Emotional resonance, scale communication, and domestic atmosphere.
- **Setting**: Authentic architect workstations, mid-century walnut desks, concrete brutalist window ledges, or library book shelves.
- **Time of Day**: Strictly **Dusk (*Godhuli*)** or late-night deep work hours. Natural fading daylight outside the window balanced against the lamp’s 2400K internal warm glow.
- **Companions & Scale Anchors**: Real architectural tools (rotring pens, fountain pens, trace paper rolls, unglazed ceramic mugs, open design monographs).
- **Human Presence**: Subtle and secondary. A hand reaching to pull the brass chain; fingers resting beside an incense tray. Never cheesy smiling lifestyle models looking directly into the camera.

### MODE C — THE MICRO-TACTILE DETAIL
- **Purpose**: Irrefutable proof of physical craft, material integrity, and mechanical joinery.
- **Focal Length**: 90mm–105mm dedicated macro lens, shallow depth of field (f/2.8 to f/4).
- **Focus Targets**:
  - The 0.16mm horizontal layer micro-ridges catching backlight.
  - The tiny entrapped air pinholes and quartz sand grain of cured concrete.
  - The 240-grit longitudinal brushing and unlacquered luster of turned C360 brass.
  - The 2mm shadow reveal separating polymer and stone.

---

## 3. COLOR GRADING & POST-PRODUCTION DISCIPLINE

- **White Balance**: Warm and filmic. Color temperature balanced to preserve the golden amber core of luminaires without turning concrete sickly yellow.
- **Saturation**: Muted and natural. Never artificially oversaturate colors. Materials should look like they do under natural diffused northern skylight.
- **Grain**: Subtle, organic 35mm film grain texture (ISO 400 equivalent) added in post to soften digital sensor sharpness and blend digital photography into architectural print paper.
- **NO False Renders**: Never substitute 3D CAD KeyShot / Blender renders for real finished production items in catalog listings. If it cannot be photographed in physical reality, it is not ready for sale.
