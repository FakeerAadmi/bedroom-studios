# 10. PRODUCT DESIGN REVIEW CHECKLIST
**The Gatekeeping Framework for New Product Approval**
*Version 1.0 — Autumn 2026*

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FRAMEWORK
When a designer or founder proposes a new object for Bedroom Studios—whether a new desk luminaire, a cement vessel, or a Bedroom Labs prototype—it must pass through this review framework.

**There are NO arbitrary numerical scores (e.g. "8/10").** Every question requires a binary **YES** or **UNACCEPTABLE**, accompanied by physical proof. If a proposal fails even a single core inquiry, it is sent back for redesign.

---

## STAGE 1: THE REASON TO EXIST (CONCEPTUAL GATE)

- [ ] **1.1 Spatial Purpose**: Does this object solve a genuine physical or atmospheric problem in the workspace, or is it merely filling empty shelf space?
- [ ] **1.2 Daily Ritual**: Can you name the exact moment of the day when a human will physically interact with this object (e.g., lighting at dusk, setting down keys, striking incense)?
- [ ] **1.3 Portfolio Distinction**: Is this object distinctly different from everything else in our catalog, or is it an unnecessary duplicate of an existing silhouette?
- [ ] **1.4 The Desk Space Test**: In a compact modern apartment, does this object truly deserve the 150mm–300mm of desk real estate it occupies every single day?

---

## STAGE 2: FORM & GEOMETRIC RIGOR (GEOMETRY GATE)

- [ ] **2.1 Geometric Honesty**: Is the form derived from structural, optical, or mechanical logic, rather than arbitrary digital sculpting?
- [ ] **2.2 Chamfers & Edges**: Are all exposed corners relieved by a 1.5mm–3mm 45° chamfer? (No razor-sharp unmachined edges).
- [ ] **2.3 The Shadow Reveal**: Where two materials meet, is there an intentional 2mm shadow reveal gap? (Zero sloppy flush seams).
- [ ] **2.4 360-Degree Intent**: Does the object look intentional, balanced, and elegant from the rear and sides, including the cable entry and switch placement?
- [ ] **2.5 Logo Removal Test**: If all logos and packaging are removed, is the object immediately recognizable as Bedroom Studios through its stepped fluting, mass, and material duality?

---

## STAGE 3: MATERIAL & MANUFACTURING HONESTY (MATERIAL GATE)

- [ ] **3.1 Material Integrity**: Is every material acting as itself? (No wood-filled plastic, no faux-gold paint, no simulated stone).
- [ ] **3.2 The Three-Material Rule**: Does the visible exterior contain three or fewer distinct materials?
- [ ] **3.3 Toolpath Celebration**: If 3D-printed, are the layer lines, micro-stepping, and internal gyroid infill intentionally used as light-diffusing optical features?
- [ ] **3.4 Mineral Consolidation**: If cast in cement, has it cured for at least 48 hours, sealed with matte penetrating siloxane, and free of structural blowouts?
- [ ] **3.5 Mechanical Disassembly**: Can the entire object be taken apart for servicing or component recycling in under 3 minutes using standard hand tools? (Zero hot glue or permanent chemical welds).

---

## STAGE 4: TACTILITY, GRAVITY & ACOUSTICS (PHYSICAL CONTACT GATE)

- [ ] **4.1 The Gravitational Test**: Is at least 65% of the total mass concentrated in the lower third of the volume? Does the base weigh over 400g (or > 850g for luminaires)?
- [ ] **4.2 The Blindfold Test**: When running fingers across every seam with eyes closed, is there zero sharp flash, rough burr, or unintended roughness?
- [ ] **4.3 The Acoustic Signature**: Do moving parts, switches, or felt-padded landings produce a deep, damped, metallic sound rather than a hollow plastic rattle?
- [ ] **4.4 Noble Ageing**: Will the brass develop an authentic handling patina and will the cement absorb the character of the room over 5+ years of use?

---

## STAGE 5: LIGHT QUALITY (FOR LUMINAIRES ONLY)

- [ ] **5.1 Zero Diode Point Glare**: Is the direct LED emitter completely concealed behind diffusing walls, baffles, or indirect reflectors?
- [ ] **5.2 Spectrum Strictness**: Is the color temperature clamped strictly between 2400K and 2700K with CRI Ra ≥ 95?
- [ ] **5.3 Flicker Free**: Is the driver certified ripple-free DC constant current (< 0.02 flicker index)?
- [ ] **5.4 Thermal Dissipation**: Does the brass/metal heat-sink maintain the LED core temperature below 55°C after 6 hours of continuous operation?

---

## APPROVAL VERDICT
- **PASSED ALL 21 INQUIRIES**: Proceed to pilot small-batch production run (10–25 units).
- **ANY FAILURE**: Return to CAD / tooling bench with specific failure note.
