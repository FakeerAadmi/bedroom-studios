## 1. THE CHROMATIC PHILOSOPHY

Bedroom Studios does not choose colors from seasonal trend palettes. Our chromatic universe is derived from three physical realities:
1. **The Mineral Earth**: Raw Portland cement, volcanic basalt, charcoal aggregate, terracotta silt.
2. **The Domestic Metallurgy**: Oxidized brass, cast bronze, dark oiled walnut, raw lead came.
3. **The Spectral Warmth of Dusk (*Godhuli*)**: The golden amber light that filters through Indian verandas between 5:30 PM and 6:30 PM.

We reject the cold, sterile stark-white plastic palettes of Northern European consumer electronics, as well as the loud, fluorescent saturated tones of mass-market gaming gadgets.

## 3. INDIAN CULTURAL COLOR: RESTRAINT OVER EXOTICISM

How does Indian cultural context inform our color without falling into tourist clichés?

- **The Myth of "Colorful India"**: Western tourists expect electric hot pink, bright turquoise, and marigold yellow. But the actual material reality of historic Indian architecture—the stepwells of Gujarat, the red sandstone of Fatehpur Sikri, the exposed concrete of Chandigarh, the stone temples of Thanjavur—is overwhelmingly **mineral, earthy, and profound**.
- **The Ochre of Brick & Mortar**: We draw on burnt brick clays (*surkhi*), dried turmeric root, and aged brass vessels (*chembu* and *urli*).
- **The Ritual of Lamp Light**: In Indian home rituals, light is generated by mustard oil and brass *diyas*—a deep, low-kelvin (2200K) amber pool surrounded by immense shadow. This is the precise atmosphere our luminaires recreate.

## 4. LIGHT CHROMATICITY AND KELVIN SPECIFICATIONS

Light is the most emotional color in our studio. We enforce strict spectral standards:

| PARAMETER | STANDARD | RATIONALE |
|---|---|---|
| **CCT (Correlated Color Temp)** | **2400K – 2700K** | 2400K evokes filament embers and evening candle glow; 2700K provides warm domestic task light. Anything above 3000K is strictly prohibited. |
| **CRI (Color Rendering Index)** | **Ra ≥ 95, R9 ≥ 80** | High R9 (deep red rendering) ensures human skin, warm timber grain, and raw brass appear rich and vibrant, not sickly or washed out. |
| **Flicker Index** | **< 0.02 (IEEE 1789-2015)** | Completely ripple-free DC constant-current drivers to prevent subliminal neurological eye strain during multi-hour night sessions. |
| **Diffusion Attenuation** | **55% – 70% transmittance** | Ensures zero bare LED point glare while allowing the interior of the shade to glow with uniform luminescence. |

## 5. PACKAGING AND DIGITAL CHROMATICS

### Packaging Palette
- **Primary Box Board**: Unbleached, 100% post-consumer recycled heavyweight kraft board (#C4B69C) with natural wood-fiber inclusions.
- **Inks**: Single-color Deep Carbon Black soy ink. No CMYK process printing, no foil stamping, no plastic UV laminates.
- **Interior Damping**: Die-cut corrugated pulp trays or molded recycled paper inserts. Zero styrofoam, zero petroleum bubble wrap.

### Digital Palette (Website & Radar)
- **Deep Room Backdrop**: `#07080B` to `#0B0C10` (never harsh pure `#000000`; always warm obsidian with blue-violet undertones).
- **Illuminated Content Sheet**: `#FAF9F5` (warm archival cream with 90% black ink typography).
- **Telemetry Accent**: `#D4FF00` (tactile phosphor green, used exclusively for live telemetry radar dots, small batch tags, and active cart pills).
