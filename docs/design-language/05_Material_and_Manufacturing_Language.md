## 2. MATERIAL COMBINATION RULES

### Rule 1: The Three-Material Ceiling
No single Bedroom Studios product may incorporate more than **three distinct materials** in its visible exterior architecture.
- *Permitted Formula*: `[Mineral Anchor] + [Optical Medium] + [Metallic Junction]`
  - Example: Cast Cement Base + Translucent Polymer Diffuser + Lathe-Turned Brass Collar.
- *Forbidden*: Adding wood trim, colorful silicone accents, and painted aluminium to an already complex assembly. Restraint produces elegance.

### Rule 2: Never Fake a Material (Anti-Imitation)
- Never print plastic with "wood filament" to simulate timber.
- Never spray-paint 3D prints with metallic chrome or gold spray.
- Never print faux-concrete stone textures. If you want cement, cast cement. If you want brass, turn brass.

### Rule 3: The Thermal Gradient
The human hand reads quality through thermal conductivity. When touching an object:
- The base should feel **cool and heavy** (mineral cement / solid bronze).
- The touch point (switch / knob) should feel **dense and metallic** (brass).
- The optical shade should feel **warm and neutral** (satin polymer).
This thermal gradient creates a multi-sensory map of the product’s internal hierarchy.

## 3. MANUFACTURING HONESTY & PROCESS TOLERANCES

### Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing)
- **Toolpaths are Graphic Features**: Infill patterns (gyroid, concentric, rectilinear) become visible internal skeletons when backlit. We tune the wall count specifically so the infill forms an architectural moiré pattern.
- **Layer Orientation**: The Z-axis build direction must always run parallel to the vertical axis of the lamp to ensure layer rings refract downward light into horizontal gradient haloes.
- **Seam Placement**: Z-seams must be programmed into rear internal vertices or masked behind vertical fluting ribs. Never leave a random z-seam zipper down the face of a curved diffuser.

### Mineral Casting (Concrete)
- **Air Voids (Bug Holes)**: Small surface pinholes (< 1.5mm diameter) are welcome signs of cast mineral reality. Large blowout pockets (> 3mm) caused by poor consolidation are rejected.
- **Curing Discipline**: All cement parts must cure in high-humidity water immersion for a minimum of 48 hours to achieve full compressive strength and prevent edge crumbling.
- **Surface Sealing**: Never apply high-gloss acrylic or polyurethane wet-look sealers. Use only penetrating matte siloxane sealers that preserve the dry, chalky, tactile stone texture while repelling oils and water.

### Brass & Bronze Machining
- **Grain Direction**: Lathe-turned brass must exhibit concentric micro-brushing; milled brass plates must feature longitudinal 240-grit linear satin grain.
- **The Living Patina**: We do not clear-coat our brass. Over weeks and months, the points of human contact will brighten while recessed crevices will darken into deep brown and amber tones. This patina documents the life of the object.
