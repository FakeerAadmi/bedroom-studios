# 09. WHAT BEDROOM STUDIOS DOES NOT DO
**The Prohibitions, Anti-Patterns, and Unacceptable Shortcuts**
*Version 1.0 — Autumn 2026*

---

## PREAMBLE
A design studio is defined as much by what it refuses to make as by what it creates. This document is a **brutally practical list of prohibitions**. If any proposed CAD file, prototype, marketing copy, or packaging concept violates an item on this list, it is immediately vetoed.

---

## 1. MANUFACTURING & PRINTING PROHIBITIONS

- **NO "Print-in-Place" Novelty Trinkets**: Never design articulated dragons, flexi-octopuses, fidget toys, or geometric wireframe puzzles. We are not a MakerWorld showcase.
- **NO Post-Print Bondo & Car Paint**: Never spray-paint 3D prints with high-gloss automotive primer and spray paint to pretend they are injection-moulded plastic. If an object is 3D printed, its layer lines must be celebrated as honest optical texture.
- **NO Wood-Filled or Marble-Filled PLA Gimmicks**: Never use plastic filaments infused with 10% wood dust or stone powder to imitate organic materials. If a design needs wood, mill real timber. If it needs stone, cast real cement.
- **NO Objects That Exist Only Because They Are Easy to Print**: Never design an object simply because it has zero overhangs and prints fast in vase mode. The design must be architecturally necessary, regardless of tooling difficulty.
- **NO Cheap Chemical Adhesives**: Never assemble parts using hot glue, superglue, or permanent chemical bonds. Assemblies must be mechanical, serviceable, and modular.

---

## 2. AESTHETIC & FORM PROHIBITIONS

- **NO Arbitrary Blobby "Digital Clay" Shapes**: No organic, melted-candle, blobby, or freehand digital sculpting without architectural geometry.
- **NO RGB Rainbow LED Modes**: No pulsing, flashing, cycling color LEDs. We build luminaires for stillness, not gaming rigs.
- **NO Cold Blue-White Light (> 3000K)**: No harsh 4000K–6500K diodes that destroy night vision and circadian rhythms.
- **NO Razor-Sharp Raw 90° Edges**: No unchamfered, razor-sharp edges that chip, catch skin, or look like unfinished CAD primitives.
- **NO Faux-Distressed / Antique Fake Patina**: Never use chemical antiquing sprays, artificial paint washes, or fake rust powders to make a new object look vintage. Use raw brass and let real human hands patina it over five years.

---

## 3. CULTURAL & STYLING PROHIBITIONS

- **NO "Exotic Indian" Tourist Kitsch**:
  - No brass elephants, peacocks, or paisleys.
  - No Mughal miniature painting decals stuck onto modern lamps.
  - No faux-royal maharaja filigree or gold-foil stamping.
  - Our Indian heritage is found in **Brutalist concrete architecture (Doshi, Le Corbusier), everyday utility brass vessels (*chembu*, *urli*), and evening sensory rituals (*godhuli*, *agarbatti*)**—never in tourist clichés.
- **NO Scandinavian Pastel Homeware Mimicry**: No powdery pinks, sage greens, or cozy beige minimalism that lacks structural backbone and architectural weight.
- **NO Generic "Luxury" Clichés**: No faux marble resin, no mirrored chrome, no velvet linings, no pretentious "luxury lifestyle" marketing language.

---

## 4. BRANDING & PACKAGING PROHIBITIONS

- **NO Giant Visible Logos**: Never print or engrave a large logo on the visible top face of a product. The brand mark should be discreetly debossed on the bottom felt pad or cast into the hidden underside. If an object requires a giant logo to be recognized, its form has failed.
- **NO Plastic Packaging Waste**: No polystyrene foam chunks, no petroleum bubble wrap, no plastic polybags, no glossy plastic tape. Packaging must be 100% recyclable unbleached corrugated fiberboard and kraft paper tape.
- **NO Empty Buzzword Copy**: Never use the words *"revolutionary"*, *"iconic"*, *"disruptive"*, *"timeless luxury"*, or *"minimalist design"* in our communication. Speak directly and factually about materials, weights, tolerances, and rituals.
